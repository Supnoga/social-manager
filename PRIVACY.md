# Privacy Policy – Social Manager

**Operator:** N2N s. r. o., Kamenná 4, Žilina, Slovakia (https://n2ntechnology.com)
**Last updated:** 13 November 2026

Social Manager is an internal, self-hosted tool used only by our own team to publish our own videos to our own YouTube channels and other social media accounts. It is not offered to the public or to other users.

## What data the tool uses
- **OAuth tokens** for the YouTube channels we own, granted by the channel owner through Google's sign-in. They are used only to upload and manage videos on those channels.
- **Our own video files, titles, descriptions, thumbnails, captions and playlists**, which we create ourselves.
- **Statistics of our own videos and channels** (views, watch time, likes, comments) from the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API, used only for our internal dashboard.
- **Comments on our own videos**, read so that our team can answer them.

## How the data is stored
All data is stored only on our own computer in a local database. It is not stored on any public server.

## Sharing
We do not sell, share or transfer any data obtained through YouTube API Services to third parties. We do not access channels or data of other YouTube users.

## Deleting data and revoking access
Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. When access is revoked or a channel is removed from the tool, its tokens and stored statistics are deleted from our computer within 30 days. Requests can be sent to us via https://n2ntechnology.com.

## YouTube and Google
This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the channel owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).
